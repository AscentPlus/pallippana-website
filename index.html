Pallippana

A Sacred Ritual Conducted Once in 12 Years

February 8 - 22, 2026

Ambalapuzha Sree Krishna Swamy Temple

History & Significance

The Ambalapuzha Sree Krishna Swamy Temple, revered as the 'Dwarka of the South', has a history spanning over 400 years. Among its many traditions, Pallippana stands out as an extremely rare and profound ritual, occurring only once every 12 years.

The term "Pana" translates to "Song", and Pallippana involves the chanting of sacred songs and the performance of ritualistic arts to purify the land and the temple premises. It is a Dravidian ritual deeply rooted in the ancient spiritual practices of Kerala.

Believed to remove all "Doshas" (impurities) that may have accumulated over time, the ritual involves the participation of traditional communities who perform specific rites, including the creation of 'Kalams' (sacred floor drawings) and the invocation of divine energy through 'Thullal' (ritual dance).

The uniqueness of this event lies in its rarity and the collective devotion it commands, drawing thousands of devotees to witness the divine unfolding.

Day-Wise Programs

Day 1: Kaappu Kettu (Flag Hoisting)

Feb 08, 2026

The sacred beginning of the festival. The 'Kaappu' (sacred thread) is tied, signifying the commencement of the vows and rituals. The presiding deity is invoked with special offerings.

Day 2: Raksha Kalasha Pooja

Feb 09, 2026

Worship involving sacred pots (Kalashas) to invoke protection ('Raksha') for the temple and the devotees. Chanting of specific mantras accompanies this rite.

Day 4: Bheeshma Sharashayanam

Feb 11, 2026

A symbolic representation and ritual offering paying homage to the ancestors and the great warrior Bheeshma. Special prayers are conducted for lineage purity.

Day 8: Kalikoodam Thullal

Feb 15, 2026

A vibrant ritual dance performance ('Thullal') dedicated to the Goddess Bhadrakali. Performers in trance-like states invoke the deity's blessings.

Day 10: Valiya Kumbha Bali

Feb 17, 2026

A major sacrificial rite (symbolic) using large gourds ('Kumbha') representing the offering of the ego and negativity to the divine.

Day 15: Valiya Kolam & Samapanam

Feb 22, 2026

The grand finale. The 'Valiya Kolam' (Great Form) is displayed or enacted. The festival concludes ('Samapanam') with the lowering of the flag and final blessings.
